# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: recruiters, talent/HR teams, and hiring managers evaluating Falih for junior developer, internship, or freelance roles. They usually arrive from a job application or LinkedIn and scan quickly — they need to judge stack, real output, and how to reach him within about two minutes.

Secondary: potential freelance clients and fellow developers who open the projects to check the quality of the work and the code.

Context: single-page scroll, mobile and desktop, often under time pressure. The job to be done for every visitor is the same — decide, fast, whether this is someone worth a conversation.

## Product Purpose

A single-page portfolio that showcases Falih's skills, projects, and working personality so that interested visitors (recruiters, clients, engineers) convert into a message or a call. Success is measured by a visitor leaving with a clear picture of what he builds and a frictionless path to contact — not by dwell time or page count.

## Positioning

A frontend-minded developer who ships real, recruiter-ready web products with AI and data curiosity. Every section reinforces the same claim: the work is polished, practical, and ready for real users.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: get in touch — mailto + contact form, reinforced by LinkedIn. Secondary CTA: view projects.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: "I build clean, real web products — and I'm ready to ship yours."
- Belief ladder (what a visitor must believe, in order):
  1. This is a real, serious person, not a template.
  2. He can build polished, working interfaces, not just mockups.
  3. His projects solve actual problems with real stack and flow.
  4. He is worth a conversation.
- Proof on hand: real project case studies (the Projects section), linked GitHub code, and live project links. No testimonials or metrics collected yet.

## Brand Personality

Bold, Confident, Playful. The neobrutalist system — hard offset shadows, loud accent colors, Space Grotesk — is the voice. The playfulness is intentional and crafted, never childish or doodly.

## Anti-references

- Generic corporate-minimal SaaS portfolios that all look like the same template.
- Bland "modern" beige placeholders with no point of view.
- Cartoonish / hand-drawn doodle illustrations passed off as personality.
- Muted, washed-out palettes that drain the energy out of the page.

(These were recommended by the design lead because the brand owner deferred the call; adjust freely.)

## Design Principles

- Show, don't tell — every claim about skill is backed by a real project or code link.
- Practice what you preach — the site itself is the strongest proof of frontend ability.
- Confidence through craft — neobrutalism with discipline, not chaos; structure underneath the loudness.
- Make the next step obvious — one clear contact path, always reachable, never buried.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target WCAG 2.1 AA.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` (already handled in globals.css).
- Keep visible `:focus-visible` states for keyboard users (present via the nb-btn / global focus ring).
- Maintain readable contrast on accent-colored text over dark sections; verify accent-on-dark pairings before shipping new sections.
